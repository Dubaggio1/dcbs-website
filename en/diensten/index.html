Tailored Solutions for Compliance Success

Three specialisations. One approach: management systems that create demonstrable, durable compliance — not one-off advice, but structures your organisation actually uses.

01 — Privacy

Privacy Compliance

Privacy compliance ensures that organisations handle personal data responsibly and in line with legal requirements such as the GDPR. We assess risks, implement governance frameworks and build management systems that make your organisation demonstrably compliant — and keep it that way.

Specialist in BC 5701 Privacy Seal certification — the recognised mark for data processors demonstrating demonstrable compliance.

Privacy Management System

A PDCA-based system: policy, procedures, processing register, monitoring — fully documented and ready for any audit.

DPO as a Service

An external Data Protection Officer — immediately deployable, meeting all GDPR Art. 37-39 requirements.

Privacy Audits & DPIA

Independent audits of your privacy programme and Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk processing activities.

Vendor & Third Party Assessments

Processor evaluation, contract review and supply chain risk management under GDPR Art. 28.

Privacy Due Diligence

Structured privacy assessment for M&A transactions — identifying hidden risks before the deal closes.

BC 5701 Certification

Full guidance through the BC 5701 Privacy Seal certification process, from gap analysis to certificate.

02 — Artificial Intelligence

AI Compliance

The EU AI Act is in force. Organisations deploying AI systems need to act now. We help you inventory your AI portfolio, classify risks and establish the required governance — from ISO/IEC 42001 management systems to Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments.

EU AI Act Implementation

AI system inventory, risk classification, conformity assessments, technical documentation and registration requirements.

AI Management System (ISO/IEC 42001)

Complete AI governance framework: risk policy, responsibilities, monitoring and continuous improvement cycles.

Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA)

Structured assessment of the impact of your AI systems on fundamental rights — required for public authorities.

GPAI Model Compliance

Transparency obligations and compliance frameworks for General Purpose AI models such as large language models.

03 — Data

Data Management

Good data governance is the invisible foundation beneath every privacy and AI compliance programme. We map your data maturity, design governance frameworks and ensure your data is accurate, accessible and aligned with regulatory requirements.

Data Maturity Assessment

A clear picture of where your organisation stands on data management — and a concrete path to maturity.

Data Governance Framework

Roles, responsibilities, policies and processes for consistent, accountable and regulation-compliant data management.

Data Quality Management

Systems and controls that ensure the accuracy, completeness and reliability of your data assets.
