When do you need this?

You are required to appoint a DPO, but do not have a full-time position
You are a start-up or scale-up and want privacy by design from the start
Someone handles privacy on the side, but there is no independent DPO
Investors or customers ask how you have arranged privacy

Good to know. The DPO is a formal role, not a hat someone wears on the side. The role is purely about privacy and is one of oversight. The operational work is done by a privacy officer, and information security and supplier management fall under separate services. That keeps the DPO independent.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is a DPO mandatory?+

For public authorities, and for organisations that process special categories of personal data on a large scale or systematically monitor people. Even when it is not mandatory, a DPO can be a smart choice.

What does it cost?+

That depends on what you need. After the intro call you receive a fixed proposal, with no surprises afterwards.
