FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference from a DPO?+

The DPO provides independent oversight. The privacy officer does the operational work. Many organisations are not required to have the first, but do need the second.

What does it cost?+

That depends on what you need. After the intro call you receive a fixed proposal, with no surprises afterwards.
