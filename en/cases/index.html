Evides Waterbedrijf · 2020 to 2023

Three years of embedding privacy at a critical drinking water supplier

Evides supplies drinking water to 2.5 million people. A critical sector with high demands on continuity and care. For three years I was its privacy consultant and interim Data Protection Officer.

The assignment: privacy not on paper, but embedded in the daily way of working. An up-to-date record of processing, DPIAs for new systems, advice the business understands and applies, and a handover to the internal organisation.

Result
Record of processing structurally up to date
DPIA practice set up and handed over
Privacy embedded in the daily way of working

“Jeroen has shown that he can turn his thorough knowledge of privacy law into workable policy and rules that people understand.”

Senior Project Manager, Evides Waterbedrijf

More assignments

Besides Evides, I have worked for, among others, the AFM (interim DPO), the City of Amsterdam (interim privacy officer for the neighbourhood teams), IKEA Supply and Ahold Delhaize (privacy compliance project management) and SCOR Digital Solutions.
