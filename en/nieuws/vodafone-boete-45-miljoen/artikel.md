The German regulator imposed a fine of €45 million on Vodafone Germany, one of the largest GDPR penalties in Germany's enforcement history. The case combines breaches of retention obligations, insufficient technical measures and a failing process for handling data subject requests.

## What went wrong

- Customer data kept far beyond the permitted retention periods
- Insufficient technical measures to enforce deletion obligations
- Systemic errors in handling erasure requests under Article 17 GDPR
- No functioning internal monitoring and audit processes

## Three lessons you can apply straight away

**1. Retention policy has to be enforced technically.** A retention schedule in a policy document is not enough. The systems have to actually delete data at the right moment.

**2. Data subject rights require operational processes.** Erasure requests have to run through a tested and monitored process.

**3. Scale is not a defence. It is an aggravating factor.** Regulators fine proportionally more heavily for systemic errors at scale.
