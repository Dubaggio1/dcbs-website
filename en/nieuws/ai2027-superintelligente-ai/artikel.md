The AI2027 scenario outlines a plausible path to transformative AI systems in 2027. Whether you find that timeline credible or not: the compliance implications are considerable and largely independent of the exact pace of development.

## Why compliance professionals need to act now

The EU AI Act is in force. Obligations for high-risk AI systems, including risk management systems, technical documentation, human oversight and post-market monitoring, apply now. AI2027 represents not only a technical scenario, but also a governance challenge.

> "The question is not whether your AI systems are compliant today. It is whether your governance structures can grow along as those systems become more capable."

## Three practical steps for forward-looking AI governance

- **Take stock of your AI systems now.** Know what you run, the risk category under the AI Act and the current oversight mechanisms.
- **Build adaptive governance.** Implement an AI management system (ISO/IEC 42001) with regular review cycles.
- **Carry out FRIAs proactively.** Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments identify how your AI systems affect people.

The organisations best positioned for the AI landscape of 2027 are those that build robust governance frameworks today.
